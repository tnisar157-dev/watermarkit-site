1 Videos and quality

Will my video lose quality?

The video is re-encoded once, at high quality. With Quality set to Original it keeps its resolution, frame rate, sound and recording date. Choose 1080p or 720p for a smaller file.

What does “Keep HDR” do?

iPhones record HDR video by default. Keep HDR saves your video as HDR, so it looks the way you shot it, and your mark appears at normal white rather than glowing. Choose “Convert to SDR” if you share to an app that shows HDR video badly. H.264 is always SDR.

Which videos can it open?

Anything your iPhone can play: videos from the Camera, screen recordings, and MOV, MP4 and M4V files. Formats iOS can’t open, such as WebM or MKV, need converting to MP4 first. Protected videos, such as Apple TV purchases, can’t be opened.

My video is in iCloud. Why is it downloading?

If your library keeps originals in iCloud, the video has to download before it can be watermarked. You’ll see the progress; you need an internet connection for this step only.

2 Exporting

Can I leave the app while it exports?

On iOS 26 and later, yes: the export carries on and iOS shows its progress. On earlier versions, keep the app open until it’s done. If you leave, the export pauses and starts again from the beginning when you come back — videos can’t resume half-way.

Where does the finished video go?

To your photo library, next to the original, unless you turned off “Save to Photos automatically” in Settings. You can also share it straight from the finished screen.

“Not enough space”

The app checks before it starts. Free up some space, or pick a lower quality on the export screen to make a smaller file.

3 Free exports and your purchase

How do the free exports work?

Your first three exports are free, at full quality and with nothing added to your video. A cancelled or failed export doesn’t count. After three, you can unlock unlimited exports with a one-time purchase. There is no subscription.

I bought the unlock on another device. How do I get it here?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase. Family Sharing is supported, so family members can use it too.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the purchase.

4 Marks and presets

Can I use my WatermarkIT presets?

Yes. If WatermarkIT is installed on the same device, its presets appear in Watermark Video. Editing one saves a copy in Watermark Video, so your WatermarkIT presets stay as they are.

My mark is hard to see in parts of the video

The timeline shows those parts underlined. Try an outline or backdrop, a darker colour, or moving the mark.

5 Privacy

Your videos never leave your device, and the app collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
