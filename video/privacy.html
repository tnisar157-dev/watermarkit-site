1 Overview

Watermark Video does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data or videos. Everything happens on your iPhone or iPad.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Watermark Video iOS application (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles your information. The App is made by Talha Nisar, who also makes WatermarkIT.

2 Data Collection

The App collects no data. It has no account or sign-in, no analytics or usage tracking, no crash-reporting service, no advertising and no server of its own. We never see your videos, your marks or anything you type.

If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics for the App. That sharing is controlled by Apple and by you, under Apple’s privacy policy, and you can turn it off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

3 How the App Works

Here is exactly what happens when you use the App:

  • You choose a video with the system photo picker, the Files app, or by dragging it in
  • The App makes a working copy of that video in its own storage on your device
  • Your marks — text, logos, signatures and QR codes — are drawn onto the video on your device
  • QR codes are generated on your device; the link you enter is never sent anywhere by the App
  • The finished video is saved to your photo library or shared through the iOS Share Sheet, as you choose
  • Working copies are deleted when you’re done with them
  • At no point is your video, or anything made from it, sent to us or to any other server

4 Device Permissions

The App asks for as little access as possible:

  • Photo library (add only) — asked the first time you save a finished video, so it can be added to your library. The App can’t read your library with this permission.
  • Choosing videos — the system photo picker and the Files app need no permission; the App only receives the video you pick.
  • Notifications — optional, only to tell you when a long export has finished or paused.

5 Location and Other Video Details

Videos recorded on iPhone can contain the place they were recorded, the date and the camera model. The App reads these on your device only. The “Remove location” switch on the export screen leaves the location out of the saved video; it is on by default. The App keeps the original recording date so the saved video sits next to the original in your library.

6 Purchases

The App is free to download and includes three free exports. Unlimited exports are a one-time in-app purchase. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple: we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The App checks your purchase with Apple’s StoreKit, and keeps the count of free exports used on your device, in its secure storage (Keychain).

7 WatermarkIT Presets

If WatermarkIT is installed on the same device, the App can show the presets you saved there. It reads them from storage that the two apps share on your device. Nothing is sent between devices or to us.

8 Data Stored on Your Device

The App keeps your presets, logos, the video you’re currently editing, a short list of recent exports and your export settings in its own storage on your device. Deleting the App removes them. The count of free exports used is kept in the Keychain so that it stays the same if the App is reinstalled.

9 Children’s Privacy

The App collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

10 Changes to This Policy

If the App’s handling of data ever changes, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new “Last Updated” date, and the App Store privacy details will be updated to match.
