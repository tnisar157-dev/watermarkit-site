Quick Help
🖼️

Add a Watermark

Tap "Pick a Photo", choose your image, then type your text or upload a logo in the editor.

📦

Batch Mode

Tap "Batch Import" on the home screen to select multiple photos and apply the same watermark to all.

🎨

Save Templates

Open Templates, tap +, design your style, then tap Export → Save as Template to reuse it anytime.

📤

Export & Share

Tap Export, choose quality (High/Medium) and format (JPEG/PNG), then save or share directly.
