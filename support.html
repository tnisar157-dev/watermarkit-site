Quick Help
🖼️

Add a Watermark

Tap "Pick a Photo" — or import from your camera or Files — then add text, a logo, a signature or a QR code in the editor.

📦

Batch Mode

Tap "Batch Import" on the home screen to select multiple photos and apply the same watermark to all.

🎨

Save Templates

Open Templates, tap +, design your style, then tap Export → Save as Template to reuse it anytime.

📤

Export & Share

Tap Export, choose your quality, format (JPEG/PNG/HEIC) and shape, then save to your Camera Roll or share directly.

Yes. Alongside the visible mark, WatermarkIT can write your name and copyright into the exported file's IPTC and TIFF fields — the standard places photo software looks for authorship. Because that data lives in the file rather than in the pixels, cropping the image does not remove it. It is your own name, never ours, and you can turn it off for any export in Settings or on the Export sheet. Note that some social platforms strip metadata when they re-encode uploads, so treat it as a strong claim to your work rather than an unbreakable lock.
