Features

Everything you need to protect your photos

Powerful tools, zero complexity. WatermarkIt does one thing and does it exceptionally well.

✏️

Text Watermarks

Add custom text with full control over font (SF Pro, Helvetica, Arial, Georgia), size, color, and opacity. Drag to reposition freely on the canvas.

🖼️

Logo Watermarks

Upload your own logo from your photo library. Scale from 10% to 200%, control opacity, and drag it anywhere on the image. PNG transparency supported.

📦

Batch Processing

Select multiple photos and apply the same watermark to all of them simultaneously. Perfect for product shoots, portfolios, and social media sets.

🎨

Reusable Templates

Save your favorite watermark styles as named templates. Built-in starter templates are included — or create and save your own for one-tap reuse.

👁️

Live Preview

Every change you make — font, size, color, opacity, position — updates the watermark preview instantly. What you see is exactly what gets exported.

📤

Flexible Export

Export as JPEG or PNG in High or Medium quality. Save directly to your Camera Roll or share via AirDrop, Messages, Mail, and more.
