Features

Everything you need to protect your photos

Four ways to mark a photo, and one mark nobody can crop off. Powerful where it counts, simple everywhere else.

✏️

Text Watermarks

22 typefaces — sans, serif, monospace, script and handwriting — with full control over size, colour and opacity. Add a shadow or an outline so your mark stays readable on busy photos.

🖼️

Logo Watermarks

Bring in your own logo and place it anywhere. Rotate a full 360°, flip it horizontally or vertically, and scale across a wide size range. PNG transparency supported.

✍️

Sign It by Hand

Draw your signature straight onto the photo in any ink colour, with undo and redo. The closest thing to signing a print, without leaving your phone.

🔗

QR Code Marks

Turn a link into a scannable mark that points at your portfolio, your shop, or your licence terms. Generated entirely on your device — no server ever sees the URL.

🧱

Tiling

Repeat your mark across the whole photo with adjustable spacing, so there is no clean corner left to crop out. The proofing standard, built in.

🪪

Invisible Credit

Write your name and copyright into the exported file's IPTC and TIFF fields. It travels with the file and survives cropping — a claim to the image even when the visible mark is gone.

⚡

Eight Built-In Presets

Named for the job, not the settings: Client Proof, Confidential, Draft, Photo Credit, Social Handle, Tiled Proof, Date Stamp and Fine Print. Tap one and you are done.

📦

Batch Processing

Select multiple photos and apply the same mark to all of them at once. Perfect for product shoots, portfolios and social media sets.

🎨

Reusable Templates

Save your own watermark styles as named templates and reuse them with one tap. Reopen anything from Recent Work on the home screen and send it out again.

📤

Export for Where It's Going

JPEG, PNG or HEIC. Shapes for 1:1, 4:5, 9:16 and 16:9 with Fit or Fill. Original, Large or Web sizes with real dimensions and an estimated file size — and name the files yourself.

How It Works

Three steps. Done.

Simple, intuitive interface. Just open the app and go.

1

Pick a Photo

Choose from your library, take a new one with the camera, or import from Files. Recent Work on the home screen brings back anything you have already made.

2

Add Your Watermark

Type your text, add a logo, sign by hand, or generate a QR code. Tap a preset to skip straight to a finished look, then tile it if you want full coverage.

3

Export & Share

Save to your Camera Roll in JPEG, PNG or HEIC — cropped to shape if you need it — or share straight out through any iOS share sheet.

New in 1.1

A mark they cannot crop off

A visible watermark can be cropped, cloned out, or blurred away. So WatermarkIT also writes your name and copyright inside the photo file itself — into the standard IPTC and TIFF fields that photo editors, stock libraries and asset managers already read.

Crop the picture, and the credit is still there. It is your name, never ours, and you can switch it off for any export.
